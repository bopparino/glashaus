## Design Specificity Verdict

**Unchanged, and it should be: authored on one page and in the writing; category-interchangeable in structure on four of six.**

A's test is the sharpest statement of it yet: relabel `/self`'s dimensions from *curiosity / neediness / warmth / trust* to *p50 / p95 / error-rate / saturation* and it is a metrics dashboard in a Swiss suit. `/system` could be a Plex server page or a Postgres admin panel. Swap `Glashaus / private · self-hosted / DAY 290` for `Acme / staging / BUILD 290` and the board head does not break.

**The design's identity is carried by its words, not its composition.** Strip the strings and the shell is a well-made generic. That is precisely the risk named on the decision card, and it remains live on the four pages nobody art-directed.

Against the two named failure modes: the ornament has **not** gone decorative — every mark still traces to real state. But A names the adjacent failure precisely: it has gone **inert**. The footer fingerprint is `display:none` under 900px, invisible on the primary device. The stamp is 55×31px carrying 25 stipple dots at 34% opacity — the mechanism is real and below the threshold of human perception at that size. And `.tick.hers`, the brass rail dot marking where she reached first, **never renders at all**: 3 of 7 `.tick` elements are not direct children of `.rail`, so the `.rail > .tick.hers::before` selector misses them. A named rule in DESIGN.md that is false in the browser.

### Deterministic scan

**The more useful result is what the detector could not see.** B found **7 of 8 undocumented hex literals unflagged** — `#5F666C`, `#98A0A6`, `#12406B`, `#EAF2F9`, `#180705`, `#6E5316`, `#F7EEDA`. The entire night palette is undocumented, and the only colour the scan caught is the false positive. Both assessments independently caught the consequence: **DESIGN.md documents `machine: #767C82`, which measures 3.73:1 on light ground and would fail AA; the shipped code uses `#5F666C` at 5.15:1.** The implementation is correct and the documentation is wrong — a doc/code drift introduced by the last fix batch and not caught by anything mechanical.

### Contrast — the fix held, and broke something adjacent

**Text contrast now passes everywhere.** B measured 44 distinct pairs per scheme: **zero below 4.5:1 in either plate.** Lowest is 5.09:1 (dark) and 5.15:1 (light). The 2.59:1 defect from run one is gone, independently confirmed by A at 9.42:1.

**But three non-text pairs fail WCAG 1.4.11 (3:1), and one is a regression from the fix itself:**

### Visual overlays

Injection preflight passed; overlay ran on 5 pages producing **193 findings** across 3 rules — 177 `undersized-ui-text`, 11 `all-caps-body`, 5 `text-overflow`. The `text-overflow` finding is the same element on every page: `span.stamp-name` overflows its container by **19px**. As in run one, the undersized-text mass is almost entirely 10.5px, the documented `label` token — the detector's 11px floor disagreeing with the design system, not drift. Worth weighing against B's measurement that **558 of 820 text elements (68%) render at ≤10.5px**.

**Overlays are NOT currently on screen** — the live server was stopped and the tab navigated away during measurement. ## Overall Impression

The batch did what it set out to do and nothing more. Error recovery went 1 → 3 and is now, in A's words, among the best-written failure states in personal software — the engine-relabel rule, the draft returned to the box, "her memory is a separate SQLite file and nothing here touched it."

And it bought three new defects, all mine, all of a type: **state asserted rather than verified.** The 404 claims you are on Today. The notice claims a fact was forgotten on a page showing no facts, and claims a backup succeeded directly above a `BACKUP FAIL` row. Both survive reload and bookmarking, because both are query-param state with nothing checking them against reality. On a product whose entire claim is that its interface tells the truth about what the software did, a confirmation that lies is worse than the silent redirect it replaced.

The single biggest opportunity is unchanged and now sharper: **no page may say something is wrong without carrying the thing that fixes it.** `/system` has exactly zero interactive elements, and three separate reassurance paths — the alarm, the chat timeout, the 500 — all terminate there.

## What's Working

**Today's information order is the product thesis rendered as hierarchy.** Dream at poster scale → what she is carrying → what she is chasing that is not for you → whether she reached first → all ten system checks compressed into one 10.5px grey line. It survives contact with a failing check. Every neighbouring product would lead with a composer or a stat row.

**Failure copy that enforces the founding refusal typographically.** `asEngine()` makes "the person is not the model" a rendering rule rather than a README claim, and the timeout genuinely hands the user's text back.

**Both plates are canonical and it survives measurement.** Every token pair clears 4.5:1 in both palettes, with the night plate carrying its own `civic-field`, `brass-field` and `on-signal` values rather than an inversion. Most "dark mode too" claims do not survive this test.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] Three regressions from the last batch — all state asserted, not verified
`/nope` renders `shell('today', …)`, so `aria-current="page"` marks TODAY while you are on a page that does not exist — the location indicator lies precisely where you are most lost. The notice plate is built from `url.searchParams` with no check against reality: `/memory?did=forgot&n=12` announces "FORGOTTEN — FACT #12 IS OUT OF HER MEMORY" above a table reading "NO FACTS YET", and `/system?did=backup` prints "backed up — her whole brain is copied" directly above a `BACKUP FAIL` row. Both persist through reload and survive as shareable links. And the `--civic-field` darkening that fixed the text pair dropped the active-nav chip fill to **1.74:1** in the dark plate.
**Fix:** give the 404 its own `page` key so nothing is marked current; verify every notice against state before rendering (fact exists and is inactive; a backup row actually appeared) and drop the params after render; re-pick `--civic-field` night against *both* the text-on-fill and fill-on-ground constraints.
**Command:** `/impeccable harden`

### [P1] Every recovery path terminates in a page with no actions
`/system` has **exactly zero interactive elements** — six nav links, the toggle, the signature, nothing else. Today's alarm has a "back up now" button; the page it sends you to does not. The chat timeout says "machinery → /system". The 500 says the same. The failing check's remedy is still the literal terminal string `glashaus backup`, on a surface whose product truth ends the terminal at setup. The app announces trouble four ways and hands her a fix once.
**Fix:** put the same `POST /backup` form on `/system` beside the failing check; give every check with a remedy an inline action; translate the remedy out of shell; add a re-run-checks control so the page can prove itself recovered.
**Command:** `/impeccable harden`

### [P1] Four of six pages are a generic admin skin, and a named rule is false in the browser
Unchanged from run one, because untouched. `.tick.hers` never renders — the rail never marks where she reached first, though DESIGN.md names it. The dream tag is brass on Today and civic blue on Journal via the `--red`→civic alias. Brass appears 12× on Today with 3 actionable, and civic links are underlined too, so "brass is also the control" is unlearnable without the doc.
**Fix:** pick one page other than Today — `/self` is the candidate, it is the thesis — and give it a composition that could only be this product. Repair `.tick.hers`. Make brass structurally load-bearing (her things sit *on* the rail, the machine's do not) rather than merely chromatic.
**Command:** `/impeccable shape`

### [P2] Destructive memory edits have no undo, only instructions
The notice says "tick 'show forgotten' below to see it, and restore it any time", then leaves you to find #12 in a 300-row table sorted by importance, unhighlighted, with no anchor — carrying an ID across three screens to undo a change to your companion's memory. `resolve` on a contradiction is irreversible with no confirmation at all. The data model already keeps its promise; only the interface does not.
**Fix:** put a `restore #12` form inside the notice plate; make `?did=forgot&n=12` auto-enable `all=1` and anchor the row; give `resolve` an undo.
**Command:** `/impeccable clarify`

### [P2] The phone breaks on the two most-used pages
Header 138px + pinned footer 94px = **232px (29%) of chrome**, leaving `main` a 580px window onto a 2,261px Today page — "Carrying" begins at **603px**, so nothing she is holding is visible without scrolling past a full screen. Nav `scrollWidth` 535 vs `clientWidth` 343: SELF and SYSTEM entirely off-screen behind an unlabeled fade. Chat keeps `96px 1fr` at every width, "reached first" overflows its column by 14px, and the composer floats above 212px of dead ground because `main` is `display:block`, making the section's `flex:1` inert. Drift detail is `<title>`-on-hover only while the legend says "hover marks for detail". 15 interactive targets measure below 44×44.
**Fix:** `main{display:flex;flex-direction:column}`; stack `chatRow` under 900px; move the toggle inline with the wordmark; wrap the nav to two rows instead of scrolling; give drift marks a tap state; stack memory into per-fact plates with the action inside the plate.
**Command:** `/impeccable adapt`

**Sam (accessibility-dependent):** zero `<h1>` on any page — the outline starts at h2; no skip link despite `main[tabindex="-1"]` sitting there as the unused target, so 7 tab stops precede content on every page; the toggle's accessible name lists the choices but never the current value, with no `aria-pressed`; brass vs civic is meaning by colour alone, both underlined; the salience meter has no text alternative or `role`; drift `<rect>` hit targets are not focusable.

## Minor Observations

- `/system`'s config **MODEL row renders completely blank** — a silently omitted value in the system whose rule is that absence is always drawn.
- `emptySlot(label, hint)` places the dashed `.fill` *between* label and hint, so at 1440px they sit **881px apart** — a helper I added last batch, working against its own purpose.
- `span.stamp-name` overflows its container by 19px on all five scanned pages.
- Empty sparklines render as flat lines with rails and no zero-state, indistinguishable from "perfectly stable" — ten stacked read as placeholder rather than drawn absence.
- Three empty-state grammars survive on `/self` alone (`emptySlot`, a bare `<p>`, plain reading text); `/system` still uses the literal string `(empty)` for log tails.
- `heroCompose()` still computes `offsets` and `redLine` under a comment describing an ornament that no longer exists; `todayPage()` destructures only `{ lines, size }`.
- `.sec::after` — the hairline that runs to the container edge, a named part of the section-head silhouette — collapses to zero width whenever the sub-label wraps, which on a phone is always. `[ DRIFT TRAJECTORIES ]` breaks across three lines with `]` orphaned.
- Native `<details>` triangles and the raw `show forgotten` checkbox are the only host-platform chrome in a system that refuses stock controls.
- Wander receipt links open `target="_blank"` with no indication they leave the app.
- The chat timestamp is `float:right` inside the reading span, so on a short final line it lands mid-paragraph.
- Section indices on Today (01–04) use the same mono-on-civic treatment as nav indices (01–06) 40px above, and collide numerically.

## Questions to Consider

1. If you deleted every string from `/self` and `/system` and showed the wireframe to a stranger, what product would they name?
2. The footer is the last thing on every page and it says `UPTIME 0D 00:08`. Peak-end says that is the note the visitor leaves on. Is a server uptime counter the right last word about a companion?
3. Three ornaments are claimed as real state; the fingerprint is hidden on the primary device, the stipple is 25 dots at 34% opacity, the rails are unlabeled. If an ornament is honest but imperceptible, is "invisible" simply the other way to fail the decorative test?
4. What would this surface look like under the rule "no page may say something is wrong without carrying the thing that fixes it"?
5. `.tick.hers` was designed, documented, and never renders. How many other named rules are true in DESIGN.md and false in the browser — and what is the doc's authority worth if nothing diffs it against the page?
6. What if `forget` were an actual undo rather than a note about one? The data model already keeps the promise.
